Fantasy Trade Generator

Uses hidden researched trade values plus roster fit to generate realistic ideas for both teams.
How much do you want to win? Help both teams searches for a near-even trade where both rosters improve. Slight win targets a 1–2% adjusted-value win for the focus team, Solid win 2–3%, Strong trade 3–5%, and Fleece 5–10%. In 2-for-1 and 3-for-2 deals, the larger package is reduced by 60 per extra player before that win percentage is calculated.
Load Sleeper, ESPN, Yahoo, or paste rosters below. QB/RB/WR/TE are valued; K and D/ST are ignored.
