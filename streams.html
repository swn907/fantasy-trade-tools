How the ranking works

This is a one-week model. Sportsbook consensus props receive the largest individual-player weight when posted; matchup and opponent defensive results are next; recent role and usage provide stability. FantasyPros weekly projections and matchup ranks are used as an outside reasonableness check when their Week 7 data is available. Quarterbacks never receive PPR points.

Sources: FantasyPros Week 7 projections • FantasyPros weekly rankings • SportsGameOdds multi-book prop consensus • ESPN game logs • Sleeper rosters and depth charts.
